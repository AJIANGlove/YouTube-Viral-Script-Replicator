Here's the real business.

Studies show this forced-walkthrough layout increases retail and dining sales by up to 60% compared to properties where shops are pushed off to the side.

The casino floor itself is designed around friction. High-traffic slots sit between you and the bathroom. The cashier cage is placed so you have to walk past table games to reach it. Even the carpet patterns — loud, busy, intentionally disorienting — are meant to slow you down and keep your eyes wandering toward the next game.

It's not a casino. It's a shopping mall where the anchor tenant is a roulette wheel.

What separated the survivors from the failures was balance sheet flexibility. If you entered the crisis with debt-to-EBITDA above 5x, you didn't make it. If you had liquidity and the ability to defer obligations, you came out intact.

The pandemic revealed the structure's fragility. Revenue stops. Costs don't. And when that gap opens, it opens fast.
